## 🏳️‍⚧️ About me

Hey! I'm **Nova**, also known as **Baummarder**.

I'm a trans and pansexual developer from Heilbronn, Germany, interested in building software, understanding systems and experimenting with technology.

- 🧑‍💻 Software development, hardware tinkering and systems programming.
- 🎮 Emulation, retro gaming and game development.
- 🔧 Reverse engineering, custom firmware and unusual hardware.
- 🌍 Fluent in German and English.
- ⚧️ Trans and pansexual.
- 🏳️‍🌈 Pronouns: **xier/xiers** — any pronouns are cool too.
- 🐾 Protogen furry.
- 🌲 Trees, cozy vibes and ambitious projects.

I enjoy learning by building things, breaking down complicated problems and figuring out how technology works underneath the surface.
